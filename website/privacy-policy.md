# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 6 August 2026**

Amblyo collects nothing about you. This page explains what that means in
practice.

---

## The short version

- There is no account, no sign-in, and no email address.
- We do not collect, transmit, sell, rent or share any personal data.
- There are no analytics tools, no advertising tools, and no third-party
  software development kits in this app.
- Amblyo makes no network requests at all, other than to Apple's App Store when
  you buy or restore a subscription.
- Everything you do stays on your device, and you can export or delete all of it
  at any time.

---

## What is stored on your device

Amblyo saves the following locally, in a database only this app can read:

- Profiles you create: a name, an optional birth year, which eye you told us is
  weaker, and whether you wear correction
- Your calibration: screen size, viewing distance, and anaglyph settings
- Your practice history: sessions, individual responses, and response times
- Your weekly check-in results
- Your settings: sound, motion, reminders, appearance

This never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and neither can anyone else.

---

## What we do not collect

- No name, email address, phone number or postal address
- No advertising identifier, and no tracking of any kind across apps or websites
- No location data
- No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access
- No crash or usage analytics beyond the anonymous diagnostics you may already
  have chosen to share with Apple in your device settings
- No device fingerprinting

Our App Store privacy label says **Data Not Collected**, and that is accurate.

---

## Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Amblyo can:

- Read your glasses or contact lens prescription, to help set up your training
- Write the time you spent practising, as mindful minutes

Health data is read only with your explicit permission, is used only inside the
app, and **never leaves your device**. It is never used for advertising and is
never shared with anyone. You can revoke this permission at any time in the
Health app, and Amblyo will keep working without it.

---

## Subscriptions

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details,
card number, or Apple Account. We receive only an anonymous confirmation from
your device that a subscription is active.

Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

---

## Children

Amblyo is designed to be used by children, with a parent or carer setting it up.

We collect no data from anyone, and that includes children. There is no
behavioural advertising, no third-party content, and no way for a child to
contact anyone through this app. Purchases and settings are protected by a
parent gate.

---

## Your controls

At any time, in Profile → Settings, you can:

- **Export everything** — a file containing all of your data, which you can keep
  or send to your eye care professional
- **Delete a profile** — removes that person's history permanently
- **Delete all data** — removes everything Amblyo has stored, permanently

Deleting the app also removes all of its data from your device. Because nothing
was ever sent anywhere, there is nothing left elsewhere to delete.
