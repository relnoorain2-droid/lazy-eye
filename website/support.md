## Before anything else — the important bit

**Amblyo is a training app. It is not a medical device and it is not a medical
treatment.** It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and it
is not a substitute for care from a qualified eye care professional.

Please see an optometrist or ophthalmologist for a proper eye examination. If
you have been prescribed glasses or contact lenses, wear them. If you have been
given a plan — including patching — follow it. Use Amblyo alongside that plan,
not instead of it.

**Do not cover or patch a child's eye without professional guidance.**

Stop and rest if your eyes feel tired, sore or strained, or if you get a
headache. Eye strain is a signal to stop, not a sign of progress.

**Amblyo is not suitable for you if you have** macular degeneration, glaucoma or
other retinal or optic nerve disease; have had eye surgery in the last three
months; have new or worsening double vision; have had sudden or recent vision
loss; or have photosensitive epilepsy.

## Frequently asked questions

### Getting started

**What do I need?**
An iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later. For the binocular exercises you also
need red-cyan anaglyph glasses — the cardboard kind sold as "3D movie glasses",
usually a few dollars for a pack. The 14 monocular exercises need no accessory
at all.

**Where do I buy red-cyan glasses?**
Any online retailer. Search for "red cyan anaglyph glasses" or "3D movie
glasses". Get the plain cardboard ones. Buy a pack — children lose them, and
the filters vary slightly between brands, so a spare is useful.

**Why does the app ask how far I sit from the screen?**
Because the size of an exercise on screen only means something if we know how
large it looks to your eye. A shape that is one degree wide at 50 cm is a
different number of pixels than the same shape at 30 cm. Getting this right is
what makes your scores comparable week to week. Try to sit at roughly the same
distance each time.

**Which eye should I select?**
The one your eye doctor identified as weaker. If you are not sure, ask them —
please don't guess. Selecting the wrong eye means the exercises train the wrong
side.

**Do I need a dark room?**
No. Use Amblyo in a normally lit room. A bright screen in a dark room causes
glare and discomfort with no benefit.

### Using the app

**How much should I do?**
Regular short sessions work better than occasional long ones. The app suggests a
daily plan and caps children at 20 minutes a day. More is not better.

**Why does it stop me after 20 minutes?**
Because sustained near work causes eye strain, and because there is no reason to
think longer sessions help. Adults can practise up to 30 minutes.

**My eyes feel tired. Should I push through?**
No. Tap the tired-eyes button in the top corner and stop. Rest, look at
something far away, and come back later or tomorrow. Fatigue is not a sign that
it is working.

**How do I turn the sound off?**
Sound is off by default. If you turned it on, there is a mute button in the top
corner of every exercise — one tap. You can also turn each sound type off
separately in Profile → Settings → Sound.

**What do the A, B and C badges mean?**
They show what kind of research supports that type of exercise. Tap any badge
for a plain-English explanation. Full detail is on our
[Evidence and Methods](https://sites.google.com/view/amblyolazyeyetraining/evidence-and-methods)
page.

**What do my scores mean?**
They are **training scores, not clinical measurements.** They are useful for
watching your own practice change over weeks. They cannot tell you your visual
acuity and they cannot replace an eye examination.

**The app says there's no clear change. Is it broken?**
No — it is being honest. Amblyo only reports improvement when the data actually
supports it. Flat weeks are normal. If nothing changes over eight weeks, the app
will suggest you speak to an eye care professional, because at that point that is
the useful next step.

**I'm colour-blind and the binocular exercises don't work.**
Red-cyan glasses rely on distinguishing red from cyan, so this is expected. The
app detects it during calibration and switches you to the monocular track, which
has 14 exercises and needs no glasses.

**Will this straighten a crossed eye?**
No. Amblyopia (lazy eye) and strabismus (a turned or crossed eye) are different
things, though they often occur together. Amblyo trains how the brain uses the
weaker eye. It does not change eye alignment. Alignment needs an eye care
professional.

### Subscriptions and billing

**What does Amblyo Pro cost?**

- Yearly — $29.99 per year, after a 7-day free trial
- Monthly — $9.99 per month
- Weekly — $2.99 per week

Prices are in US dollars and may differ in your region.

**Is anything free?**
Yes. Four exercises, calibration, all safety features and the Learn section are
free forever, with no ads and no account.

**How do I cancel?**
Open **Settings** on your device → tap your name → **Subscriptions** → **Amblyo**
→ **Cancel Subscription**. At least 24 hours before your period ends.
**Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.**

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to **reportaproblem.apple.com** and sign in with
the Apple Account you used.

**I paid but the app still says Free.**
Open Amblyo → Profile → Subscription → **Restore Purchases**. If that does not
work, make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you purchased
with, then email us.

**Can my family share one subscription?**
Yes. Amblyo Pro supports Family Sharing.

### Privacy and data

**What data do you collect?**
None. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. Amblyo
makes no network requests except to Apple's App Store for purchases. Everything
stays on your device. See our
[Privacy Policy](https://sites.google.com/view/amblyolazyeyetraining/privacy-policy).

**How do I export or delete my data?**
Profile → Settings → Data. You can export everything or delete it permanently.
Deleting the app also removes all of its data.

**Can I show my progress to my eye doctor?**
Yes — export your history from the Progress screen and bring it to your
appointment.

### Troubleshooting

**The binocular exercises look ghosted.**
Run the anaglyph calibration again (Profile → Calibration). Different brands of
glasses have slightly different filters. If ghosting persists, try a different
pair — cheap filters vary a lot.

**Exercises pause when I switch windows.**
That is intentional. An exercise running in a background window produces
unreliable timing, and unreliable timing produces meaningless scores.

**The app doesn't write a plain-language summary on my device.**
The written coach summary uses Apple's on-device intelligence, which needs an
iPhone 15 Pro or newer, or an iPad with an M1 chip or newer. On other devices you
get the same information in a simpler format. **No exercise, measurement or paid
feature is affected** — only the wording of the weekly note.
