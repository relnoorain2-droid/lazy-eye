# Subscription Terms

Amblyo Pro is an optional subscription. The core of the app — four exercises,
calibration, safety features and the Learn section — is free forever, with no
ads and no account.

---

## Plans

- **Yearly — $29.99 per year**, after a 7-day free trial. About $0.58 a week.
- **Monthly — $9.99 per month.** About $2.31 a week.
- **Weekly — $2.99 per week.**

Prices are in US dollars and may differ in your region. Local taxes may apply.

## What Pro unlocks

- All 32 exercises across the monocular, binocular and games tracks
- All four weekly check-in measures
- Your complete progress history and trends
- The on-device coach
- Up to five profiles

## How billing works

- Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
- **Your subscription renews automatically** unless you turn off auto-renew at
  least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
- Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the
  current period.
- The free trial is available on the yearly plan only, for new subscribers. If
  you do not cancel before it ends, you will be charged $29.99 and the
  subscription begins.
- Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited if you buy a subscription
  before the trial ends.

## How to cancel

Cancel any time, at least 24 hours before your period ends:

1. Open **Settings** on your device
2. Tap your name at the top
3. Tap **Subscriptions**
4. Tap **Amblyo**, then **Cancel Subscription**

You can also tap **Manage Subscription** in Amblyo under Profile → Subscription.

**Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.** You must cancel through
your Apple Account.

## Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Request one at
**reportaproblem.apple.com** with the Apple Account you purchased with.

## Family Sharing

Amblyo Pro supports Family Sharing, so one subscription covers your family
group. This matters if more than one person in your household is training.
