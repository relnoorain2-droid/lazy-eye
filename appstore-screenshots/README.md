## THE ORDER, AND WHY

Most people never scroll past the third screenshot. So the first three carry the
whole pitch:

1. **32 exercises for lazy eye** — what it is, and the number that beats every
   competitor listing.
2. **18 exercises need BOTH eyes** — the thing no cheap competitor does. Also
   sets the expectation about red-cyan glasses *before* download, which prevents
   the one-star review that starts "it doesn't work without glasses".
3. **Games children actually finish** — the buying decision for a parent.

Then the mechanism (4, 5), the daily habit (6), the honesty (7) and the family
plan (8).

## THE COPY IS CHECKED, NOT JUST WRITTEN

`make_screenshots.py` runs every caption against the **same banned-word list**
as the in-app claims linter (`scripts/lint_claims.py`), and refuses to render if
anything trips it. App Review reads screenshot text under Guideline 1.4.1 exactly
like in-app text — one "treatment" or "improves your vision" in a caption is a
rejection, and a rejection costs a week.

That is why the captions say "exercises" and never "therapy", and why nothing on
these panels promises an outcome.

## WHAT WAS CLEANED UP FROM THE RAW CAPTURES

**`◀ TestFlight` was in the top-left of every single one.** That is a screenshot
of a beta distribution channel; it tells a reviewer the images are not of the
shipping app. Removed.

Several captures also froze a half-scrolled Start button over the status bar,
which reads as a rendering fault rather than a scroll position.

Both are gone: the top strip is cropped and a neutral status bar is drawn in its
place, showing 9:41 — Apple's own convention in every marketing image they
publish.

## TWO HONEST WEAKNESSES

**1. The Today panel is showing an empty app.**
That capture was taken on a fresh install, so it reads "Finish setting up" with
0 minutes, 0 days and 0%. The message is one of the strongest here and the
picture undersells it badly, which is why it was moved from second place to
sixth.

**Worth re-taking:** complete setup, run one session, then capture Today again.
Drop it into `screenshort/` as `IMG_0300.png` and re-run the script — a real
streak and real minutes in that ring would justify promoting it back to #2.

**2. The iPad panels contain iPhone captures.**
The layout is correct for the iPad slot and it will upload, but the screen inside
the device is the iPhone build. Apple's guideline 2.3.3 asks that screenshots
show the app as it appears **on that device**, and this app genuinely has a
different iPad layout — a `NavigationSplitView` with a sidebar rather than a tab
bar.

Nobody has captured that yet. If you can run the TestFlight build on an iPad,
even briefly, eight captures dropped into `screenshort/ipad/` would let this be
done properly. Until then this set is a calculated risk rather than a clean pass,
and I would rather you knew which it was.
